About us

Welcome to Regulance Consulting Services Private Limited, a leading provider of comprehensive HR compliance services with a focus on employment law advisory and compliance. We specialize in establishment compliance, factory compliance, contract Labour compliance, industrial licensing, payroll processing, and payroll compliance. Additionally, we offer consultation and legal opinions. With a presence across India, we are recognized experts in the complexities of labor laws.
